HUTTON NAVY

From 2–0 down to a brilliant Navy win!

Another Saturday morning and another league outing for the Navy boys, this time at Flints Sports Ground against a strong and physical Herongate side.

It was an intense start on the slightly smaller pitch, with both teams trying to get the ball down and play. Theo G was straight into action finding space in midfield while Bobby worked hard down the flank, but Herongate’s size and direct running made the opening exchanges difficult for Hutton.

A long ball forward eventually opened up the Navy defence and Herongate took the lead, before a corner was converted shortly afterwards to make it 2–0.

It was looking like it was to be a tough morning.

But the boys had other ideas!

Teddy produced the perfect response with an unbelievable finish direct from a corner, only for VAR to rule it out as you can’t score directly from a corner. It was some strike though!

Hutton came out a completely different side after the break - Marshall and Fraser set the tone sniffing out the danger brilliantly at the back and helping give the boys the platform to push forward.

A loose pass from the Herongate keeper fell to Alfie who reacted quickest and fired narrowly over. Hutton were getting closer….

Then came the breakthrough.

Theo Mc took the initiative from a corner, dribbling the ball into play before firing home to make it 2–1!

The Navy boys looking inspired continued the pressure. Theo G ruthless robbed the ball in Herongate and pinged it to Teddy who ran at the defence and got his shot away. Another corner presented Hutton with their opportunity. Theo Mc drove the ball hard across goal and Alfie showed his fox-in-the-box instincts to turn it home.

2–2! ⚽️

What had looked like a difficult morning was suddenly a brilliant contest. Herongate responded quickly though with a powerful run and finish to restore their lead at 3–2, but Hutton refused to let their heads drop.

Fraser produced a superb tackle to stop another attack, while Marshall continued to anticipate danger and snuffed out a clever Herongate corner routine before it could develop. The long pass down the wing found Alfie at the other end, who dribbled confidently and calmly slotted home for his second of the morning.

3–3! ⚽️⚽️

But boys were not settling for a draw - they had the bit between their teeth now! From a throw-in, Teddy drove forward with the ball and finished to put Hutton ahead for the first time….4–3!

From 2–0 down, Hutton had turned the game on its head. Now a different challenge was presented, to protect the lead and every one of the boys stood up.

Marshall showed his defensive prowess, first breaking up another attack producing a brilliant double challenge before driving the ball forward.

Fraser was called into action again and produced an unbelievable fingertip save from distance, pushing the ball onto the post, before making another brave diving save from the rebound.

Bobby’s work rate was outstanding. He tracked all the way back to help defensively before taking a throw, driving forward and playing a lovely ball into Teddy. Teddy linked up neatly with Theo G and only a strong save from the Herongate keeper prevented Hutton extending their lead. So unlucky!

Bobby was at it again shortly after - producing one of his (now trademark!) runs down the wing, cutting inside and flashing a great effort just past the far post.

The final quarter was a very even contest with Herongate pushing hard for an equaliser.

The Navy boys matched them every step of the way though - Alfie set the tone pressing from the front, Teddy continued to carry a threat on the break and behind them Marshall was immense reading the game putting in challenge after challenge. It was a tense final 5 but the boys held on to their well deserved lead to see the game to its close.

FULL TIME: HERONGATE 3–4 HUTTON NAVY

Another fantastic performance from the boys in a different type of challenge today. After going 2–0 down in the first quarter, it would have been very easy for heads to drop against a bigger and physical Herongate side. Instead, Hutton stood up to be counted. They fought their way back into the game, played some brilliant football to turn it around and then, when they got themselves in front, protected that lead with their lives! ⛴️

19.09.26 · Hutton Navy 3–4 Danbury — League Match 2

The game started with high intensity with Theo G starting between the sticks being called straight into action with a good save. It was clear from the opening minutes that Hutton were in for a game today! The first opportunity for Hutton came from a throw with Fraser finding Teddy in space - he was inches away from opening the scoring with his effort drifting narrowly wide.

Danbury responded quickly though, a scrappy goalmouth scramble eventually fell their way to make it 0–1. Quickly after a misplaced pass at the back was punished to make it 0–2.

The Navy boys weren’t quite at the races during the opening 10 mins with most of game being played in their half - Theo Mc and Fraser were battling hard against a Danbury side who had started really brightly.

Jack delivered the hairdryer treatment at the end of the first quarter and whatever was said certainly worked!

The Navy boys came back out with much more intensity. The pressing improved, tackles were now flying in.

Theo G was straight into the action after coming out of goal, he showed great hustle in midfield to win possession before getting a shot away that was well gathered by the keeper.

Alfie then won a free kick and stepped up to take it himself, firing narrowly wide. Moments later, the ball dropped to him again from the resulting goal kick. Alfie set himself and absolutely thundered an effort towards goal, only to see it fly just past the post.

A much better second quarter, but Hutton still trailed by 2 goals going into half time.

The second half began with a lovely Hutton kick off routine, Theo G to Theo Mc to Bobby showing exactly the sort of quick passing football the boys are capable of. Bobby’s resulting effort went agonisingly wide.

Now the pressure was building, Bobby’s relentless pressing caused problems for the Danbury defence and the loose ball dropped to Marshall, who fired just wide. Marshall who had made an instant impact in midfield continued to break up attacks from Danbury and was really helping Hutton play forward.

Bobby reacted quickest to a loose ball from the Danbury goal kick and hit a brilliant strike, only to be denied by an excellent save. Surely a goal was coming…!

A great tackle from Bobby in midfield won possession before he threaded a lovely ball through to Teddy. Teddy took his chance brilliantly, smashing it in off the post! 1-2

Navy were now looking in the mood!

Marshall curled a fantastic long range effort towards goal that looked destined for the net before the Danbury keeper somehow parried it wide.

Then Marshall was at it again…..a brilliant sliding tackle in midfield won the ball before he picked out Teddy. Teddy did the rest…back of the net and 2-2…Game on!!

The boys were really in it now, Bobby won an excellent duel on the wing using his strength the shrug of the Danbury player, he then linked up brilliantly with Marshall. The ball eventually found Teddy and he fired home to complete his well deserved hatrick! ⚽️ ⚽️ ⚽️

From 0–2 down to 3–2 up, the Navy boys had turned the game around but there was still work to do…Danbury were refusing to give up without a fight!

Danburys talented winger went on a run down the touchline, whipped a dangerous cross into the middle which was finished off well. 3–3 and the game was in the balance!

Danbury began piling on the pressure, a strike went crashing against the Hutton post and was well cleared by Alfie. Phew!

It was end to end….Alfie whipped in a great corner Bobby linked up well with Marshall who looped a shot from distance just wide of the post.

Danbury broke quickly from the resulting goal kick down the flank again, whipped a dangerous ball across goal and finished clinically again to bring them back into lead in the final minute of this rollercoaster of a game. 3-4

The Navy boys came desperately close in the closing stages with Alfie but the keeper did a great job parrying his shot wide.

A tough game against a strong team of Year 3 boys but a performance the boys can take plenty from. After a difficult opening quarter, the Navy boys response was fantastic and showed great character to bring the game back. On another day, a few of those efforts that flew narrowly wide go in and it’s a completely different result!

12.09.26 · Hutton Navy 6–6 Brentwood Storm — League Match 1

A beautiful sunny morning greeted the boys for their first league match of the season, with the added treat of getting to play on the sublime pitch at Brentwood Town FC.

The game certainly started with a bang. Straight from kick-off Brentwood showed Hutton they were in for a proper contest, breaking forward and scoring with virtually the first move of the game to make it 1–0.

The Navy boys didn’t panic though. They regrouped immediately and started moving the ball around really nicely on the pristine pitch. Bobby set the tone with a great tackle out wide before pinging a ball across to Teddy, whose effort was brilliantly kept out by the Brentwood keeper.

Theo Mc was next to win the ball back with a strong challenge, sending Bobby away with a long pass. Bobby drove at the defence and let fly from distance, narrowly missing the target.

The equaliser wasn’t far away. Marshall showed great determination to chase back and win the ball with a sliding tackle in midfield, before getting to his feet and fizzing a superb through ball into Bobby. Bobby showed great composure, finishing calmly to make it 1–1.

Hutton were growing into the game and Fraser got involved with a big challenge in midfield before finding Bobby on the wing. Bobby’s long-range effort was parried by the keeper, but Alfie reacted quickest to tuck away the rebound and put Navy 2–1 ahead.

There was some lovely football being played now by the Hutton boys with Bobby, Theo G and Alfie combining brilliantly. Alfie almost finished off one a great move with an absolute screamer from distance, only for the post to deny him.

Brentwood weren’t going anywhere though. Their dangerous number 2 produced a fantastic long-range strike to make it 2–2, before another goal edged them 3–2 ahead.

Hutton continued to play really well despite going behind. Theo Mc and Fraser worked tirelessly defensively, while Marshall was excellent winning duels in the middle of the park. The sustained pressure eventually paid off…..Alfie picked the ball up, dribbled past two Brentwood players and calmly slotted home to bring Navy level again at 3–3.

Then came one of the moves of the match. Theo G made a brilliant tackle and found Teddy, who exchanged passes with Alfie. Alfie drove forward and got his shot away, but Theo G had continued his run the entire length of the pitch. When the rebound dropped, he was there and thundered it into the net! 4–3 to Hutton

With the light wind behind them now, Navy really turned the screw and spent long periods camped inside the Brentwood half. But Brentwood remained dangerous on the break and their No. 2 once again raced clear to finish well and make it 4–4.

Back came Hutton again. Teddy delivered a corner into Alfie, who absolutely rocketed it into the net for his Hatrick and restored the lead! 5–4 to Hutton.

Theo G produced another strong challenge in midfield and released Teddy, his pivot and powerful effort was juggled by the keeper and once again Alfie was quickest to react, putting Hutton 6–4 ahead completing a brilliant four-goal haul for him.

But there was still more drama to come — and Alfie’s contribution wasn’t finished….this time at the other end of the pitch. Taking his turn in goal, he produced a fantastic save from a long-range free kick, before making another superb stop when Brentwood broke through and fired from distance.

Brentwood kept pushing and pulled one back to make it 6–5 bringing this cracking game to a tense final few minutes…

The boys dug deep and didn’t stop pushing to extend the lead but a goal mouth scramble following a Brentwood corner 2 mins from time led to Brentwood finding an equaliser. 6–6.

Teddy came agonisingly close in the dying seconds to winning it and getting the goal he so well deserved but was denied by a superb save from the Brentwood keeper.

FULL TIME: Hutton Navy 6–6 Brentwood Storm
